In a historic decision that sent waves through Hollywood and the global entertainment industry, billionaire producer, writer, director, and actor Tyler Perry announced his upcoming relocation to Puerto Rico in early 2027.

Speaking candidly in an interview on NBC's TODAY with Jenna & Sheinelle alongside special correspondent Justin Sylvester, Perry revealed that after decades of relentless output, he is ready to transform his lifestyle and step back from daily grueling production schedules.

"I am officially moving to Puerto Rico," Perry shared. "It’s time to slow down. I’ve been working non-stop since I can remember, and I want to enjoy the fruits of my labor while being fully present for my son."

The announcement represents a momentous shift for one of the most prolific creators in entertainment history. Known for personally writing, directing, and producing dozens of television series and feature films simultaneously, Perry's move marks the beginning of a fresh, more reflective chapter.

2. Why Now? Workload Strain, Age 57, and Fatherhood

Fatherhood as the Core Priority

At the center of Perry’s choice is his son, Aman Tyler Perry. Perry has frequently spoken about his desire to give his child a peaceful, grounded, and joyful upbringing away from the chaotic pressure of Hollywood boardrooms and red carpets.

Moving to Puerto Rico provides a natural sanctuary—combining top-tier privacy, sun-soaked natural landscapes, and a calmer rhythm ideal for family development.

Overcoming Work Fatigue & Delegating Control

For over twenty-five years, Tyler Perry's schedule has been legendary across the media ecosystem:
* Authoring and directing over 1,000 television episodes.
* Managing multi-million-dollar film franchises, including the famous Madea universe.
* Spearheading distribution partnerships with BET, Netflix, and Amazon MGM Studios.

Perry acknowledged that maintaining such an aggressive pace as he reaches age 57 is neither necessary nor healthy. Establishing early 2027 as his move date allows a clear multi-year runway to delegate key studio management tasks and transition day-to-day creative burdens to trusted team members.

3. What Happens to Tyler Perry Studios in Atlanta?

Industry observers initially wondered whether Perry's move meant liquidating or shuttering his Atlanta production headquarters. The answer is a firm no.

Spanning 330 acres on the historic site of Fort McPherson in Atlanta, Georgia, Tyler Perry Studios (TPS) is one of the largest film production facilities in the United States, featuring 12 massive sound stages and specialized outdoor backlots.

The Operational Strategy Moving Forward

  • Expanded Executive Leadership: Operational command of physical production facilities will shift to an upgraded slate of senior studio executives.
  • Remote Creative Oversight: Leveraging high-speed digital tools and cloud workflows, Perry can review scripts, examine rough cuts, and greenlight concepts directly from Puerto Rico.
  • Third-Party Stage Rentals: The Atlanta sound stages will continue hosting major blockbuster productions from Marvel, Disney, and independent studios, generating steady revenue independent of Perry's physical presence.

4. Life in Puerto Rico: Culture, Privacy & Act 60 Incentives

Puerto Rico has rapidly become a high-end destination for top entrepreneurs, artists, and business leaders. Perry's decision reflects both lifestyle aspirations and sound economic planning.

World-Class Lifestyle and Privacy

With luxury coastal enclaves such as Dorado Beach and Bahia Beach, Puerto Rico offers high-level security, pristine oceanic views, and a warm climate. For a high-profile figure like Perry, the island provides a peaceful retreat without sacrificing access to modern amenities.

Tax Advantage Structure (Act 60)

While personal health and family are Perry’s primary motivators, relocating to Puerto Rico offers significant economic advantages through U.S. tax framework provisions under Act 60:
* Export Services Incentive: Businesses providing services outside Puerto Rico can qualify for a reduced 4% corporate tax rate.
* Capital Gains Exemptions: Long-term residents enjoy significant tax savings on capital gains accrued after establishing official island residency.
* U.S. Territory Simplicity: Because Puerto Rico is a U.S. territory, American citizens relocate without needing foreign visas or passport changes, maintaining straightforward financial operations under U.S. federal laws.

Tyler Perry’s relocation reflects a broader macroeconomic trend across media and entertainment:

  2. Prioritizing Mental Longevity: Top-tier creators are rejecting continuous burnout in favor of sustainable, curated artistic output.
  3. Rise of Sunbelt & Island Tech Hubs: Locations offering strong lifestyle benefits and tax incentives continue to attract top talent and capital.
