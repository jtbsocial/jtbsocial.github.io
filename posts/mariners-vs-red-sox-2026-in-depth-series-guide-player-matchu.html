Ballpark Analytics: Fenway Park vs. T-Mobile Park

Stadium environments play a massive role when these teams square off. The physics of baseball flight differ dramatically between New England's urban landmark and the Pacific Northwest's retractable-roof stadium.

Fenway Park (Boston, MA)

  • The Green Monster (Left Field): Stands 37 feet 2 inches high, turning high fly balls that would be outs elsewhere into sharp wall-doubles.
  • Pesky's Pole (Right Field Line): Just 302 feet away, giving pull-hitters a short porch for home runs.
  • Wind & Weather: Early-season games feature cold Atlantic winds that suppress ball carry to right-center field.

T-Mobile Park (Seattle, WA)

  • Marine Layer Air Density: Cool evening air from Puget Sound increases aerodynamic drag, reducing overall hit distances by 3% to 5% compared to league average.
  • Deep Alleys: Vast outfield space rewards speed and range over raw home-run power.
  • Retractable Roof Integration: Protects games from Pacific rain while maintaining ambient outdoor temperature controls.
