For decades, stealth and tactical action games relied on predictable guard patrol paths, simple vision cones, and telepathic guard networks. ARREST incinerates those outdated mechanics and introduces the Sentinel-AI Engine.

In ARREST, law enforcement units do not simply spawn on top of you when an alarm trips. Instead, city dispatchers analyze real-time audio footprints, surveillance cameras, ballistics vectors, and witness reports. Police respond proportionately based on regional heat levels:

  1. Level 1 (Patrol Response): Standard beat cops conduct cordons and investigate suspicious noise or property damage.
  2. Level 2 (Tactical Containment): SWAT squads deploy tear gas, flashbangs, breaching charges, and perimeter snipers.
  3. Level 3 (Military/High-Tech Lockdown): Autonomous hunter-killer drones, EMP blockades, and heavy armor units lock down entire city districts.

What makes this terrifyingly realistic is the AI's collective memory. If you frequently use thermal optics and silencers, tactical squads will equip smoke grenades, infrared scramblers, and ballistic shields on future encounters. Every mistake leaves a footprint, turning every mission into a living chess match.

🎮 Core Gameplay Mechanics: Escape, Negotiate, or Engage

ARREST rejects the traditional binary choice of 'pure stealth' versus 'guns blazing.' Instead, it introduces a dynamic Escalation & De-escalation Matrix.

1. The Threat & Heat System

Your tactical actions generate ambient noise, structural destruction, and casualty counters. High heat triggers aggressive tactics, while non-lethal stealth keeps response teams off-balance.

2. Physicality & Environmental Manipulation

Every door, window, utility panel, and light fixture is interactable. Players can splice power grids to blind security cameras, weld doors shut to choke tactical choke points, or use dynamic voice modulation to trick dispatch radios.

3. The Arrest Mechanism

Unlike standard shooters where defeat means instant death, getting cornered in ARREST triggers high-stakes surrender and detention mechanics. Players can attempt to negotiate hostages, bribe corrupt commanders, execute a daring flashbang getaway, or trigger a full breakout mission from police transit units.
