1. Who is Cal Raleigh? Bio & Career Origins

Born in Cullowhee, North Carolina, Caleb John Raleigh grew up steeped in baseball culture. His father, Todd Raleigh, was a former collegiate catcher and head baseball coach at Western Carolina and Tennessee, instilling in Cal an advanced technical understanding of game management from an early age.

After a standout collegiate career at Florida State University—where he hit .326 with 13 home runs in his junior season—Raleigh was selected by the Seattle Mariners in the 3rd round of the 2018 MLB Draft. He quickly surged through the farm system, demonstrating rare switch-hitting pull power and high-level pitch calling.
