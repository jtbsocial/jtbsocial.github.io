Core Architecture & Gameplay Mechanics

To understand Muerte's dominance, one must first master the fundamental mechanics that define its kit and output.

1. Ethereal Phase Shift

Allows temporary passage into an intangible state, rendering traditional physical damage ineffective while enhancing energy damage output by up to 35%. Timing this ability perfectly negates incoming ultimate abilities.

2. Spectral Deadshot

Direct-target precision strike that applies a lingering slowdown effect while projecting a ghost trajectory. When combined with vision warding, it allows for blind picks through terrain.

3. Soul Resonance (Passive)

Every target eliminated within a 15-meter radius grants a stacking damage buff that enhances critical strikes. In extended team fights, a fully stacked Soul Resonance makes Muerte virtually unkillable.

4. Calling of the Void (Ultimate)

Summons an expansive domain that silences enemies caught in the center while granting maximum movement speed and lifesteal to Muerte. It serves as the premier zoning tool in the 2026 patch.
