When you talk about collegiate volleyball dominance, one program stands tall in the national consciousness: the Nebraska Cornhuskers. Nebraska volleyball is a cultural phenomenon in the Midwest and across the nation, boasting legendary sellout streaks at the Bob Devaney Sports Center and world-record attendance benchmarks like the historic Volleyball Day in Nebraska.

As the 2026 NCAA Volleyball season approaches, Head Coach John Cook and his staff have assembled a squad built for national championship contention. With a defense known for relentless floor coverage and an offense operating with lightning-fast tempo, every game on the 2026 Husker volleyball schedule represents a high-stakes event.

Whether you are a lifelong season ticket holder, a student in the Red Zone, or a distant fan streaming from home, understanding the flow of the 2026 schedule is vital to tracking Nebraska's quest for another national championship ring.

2026 Season Schedule Overview

The 2026 schedule follows the traditional NCAA women's volleyball timeline, kicking off in late August with non-conference multi-team events (MTEs) and classic home-and-home series, before transitioning into a 20-game Big Ten conference slate running from late September through late November.

Season Timeline at a Glance:

  • Late August: Preseason Exhibition & Opening Weekend Tournament
  • Early to Mid-September: Marquee Non-Conference Big Ten/SEC/ACC Challenges
  • Late September: Big Ten Opener
  • October: Mid-Season Grind (Key Coast-to-Coast Road Trips)
  • November: Senior Night & Big Ten Championship Deciders
  • December: NCAA Tournament & National Championship

Non-Conference Showdowns

Coach Cook has never shied away from elite non-conference opponents. The 2026 non-conference slate is designed to build a strong RPI and prepare the squad for the physical demands of postseason play.

Highlighted Non-Conference Fixtures

  1. The AVCA Showcase: Nebraska opens the season in a neutral-site showcase featuring four top-10 teams. This early test provides an immediate gauge of team chemistry and new lineup combinations.
  2. The In-State Rivalry vs. Creighton: The annual battle against Creighton remains a staple of Nebraska sports culture. The Bluejays bring high-powered offense and fierce energy, making this instate clash a must-watch matchup.
  3. ACC/SEC Power Series: Matchups against traditional powerhouses like Texas, Louisville, or Stanford highlight the non-conference home slate at the Devaney Center, offering fans high-caliber volleyball before league play begins.

The Big Ten Gauntlet

With the expansion of the Big Ten to 18 teams—incorporating West Coast giants UCLA, USC, Oregon, and Washington—the Big Ten volleyball slate is challenging every week. Every weekend features top-25 matchups and hostile environments.

Key Regional & National Rivalries:

  • Wisconsin Badgers: The premier rivalry in college volleyball over the past decade. Battles between Nebraska and Wisconsin routinely feature sellout crowds, national TV broadcasts, and high-level athletic rallies.
  • Penn State Nittany Lions: A classic rivalry rooted in decades of NCAA dominance. Matches against Penn State feature physical net play and strategic coaching adjustments.
  • Minnesota Golden Gophers: Always a tactical test, Minnesota brings disciplined defense and powerful wing attackers into the Devaney Center.
  • The West Coast Swing: Road trips to Los Angeles (UCLA/USC) or the Pacific Northwest (Oregon/Washington) add unique travel dynamics and stylistic clashes to the mid-season schedule.

How to Watch, Stream, and Attend

Securing access to Nebraska Volleyball matches requires a quick guide to broadcasting schedules and venue ticketing.

Television & Streaming Channels:

  1. Big Ten Network (BTN): The primary home for Nebraska conference games and weekly studio coverage.
  2. FS1 & FOX: Select marquee weekend matchups air on national network television.
  3. Peacock & NBC Sports: Direct streaming coverage for select Big Ten fixtures.
  4. Big Ten Plus (B1G+): Streaming platform for non-linear broadcasts, early-season non-conference events, and select exhibition games.
  5. Husker Sports Radio Network: Audio broadcasts available on local radio affiliates and via the official Husker App.

Attending Games at the Devaney Center:

  • Ticket Availability: Tickets sell out quickly. Fans are advised to monitor the official Huskers.com ticket portal for secondary market releases or standing-room-only options.
  • Doors & Clear Bag Policy: Doors open 90 minutes prior to first serve. The Devaney Center enforces a strict clear-bag policy for all attendees.
