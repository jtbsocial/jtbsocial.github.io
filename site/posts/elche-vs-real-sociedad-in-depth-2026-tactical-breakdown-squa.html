Head-to-Head Tactical Analysis

Real Sociedad: The Basque Blueprint (Possession & High Press)

Real Sociedad's setup centers on dominating space through high possession retention and intense counter-pressing within 5 seconds of ball loss.

  • Primary Formation: 4-3-3 Attack / 4-2-3-1 Wide
  • Build-Up Style: Short, patient build-up playing out through inverted full-backs.
  • Pressing Trigger: Immediate high-press upon losing possession in the attacking third.
  • Offensive Focus: Creating 2v1 overloads on the right wing through Takefusa Kubo and overlapping full-back runs.

Elche CF: The Defensive Fortress & Transition Machine

Elche focuses on structural integrity, choking central passing lanes, and punishing overcommitted opponents via rapid vertical wing play.

  • Primary Formation: 5-3-2 Flat / 3-4-1-2 Counter
  • Build-Up Style: Direct passing to target forward, combined with immediate secondary runs from wing-backs.
  • Pressing Trigger: Mid-block trap triggered when opponent passes into the central midfield circle.
  • Offensive Focus: Exploiting space down the flanks during opponent defensive transitions.
