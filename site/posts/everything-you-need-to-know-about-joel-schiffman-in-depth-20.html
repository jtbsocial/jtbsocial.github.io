1. Early Career and Kidder, Peabody & Co.

Schiffman honed his foundational skill set at Kidder, Peabody & Co., a historical Wall Street investment bank. As a Senior Vice President in the Consulting Services Division, he worked directly with institutional clients, high-net-worth families, and corporate retirement plans.

2. Strategic Expansion at Prudential & Neuberger Berman

Expanding his reach in institutional distribution, Schiffman transitioned to senior roles at Prudential Financial and Neuberger Berman. At Neuberger Berman, he served as Managing Director, overseeing national distribution efforts and cultivating multi-million dollar relationships with national broker-dealers, RIA networks, and bank trust platforms.

3. Leadership at Nuveen Investments

Schiffman’s most prominent corporate chapter unfolded at Nuveen Investments, one of the largest and most respected asset managers globally. As Senior Vice President and Head of Strategic Accounts / Financial Institutions, Schiffman was responsible for:
* Driving multi-billion-dollar distribution strategies across mutual funds, closed-end funds, and separately managed accounts (SMAs).
* Managing corporate partnerships with major wealth management platforms.
* Advising financial advisors on macro market trends, municipal bond allocations, and alternative investment strategies.

The Public Spotlight: Relationship with Kathie Lee Gifford

While Schiffman was well known in financial circles, his entry into public celebrity status occurred in 2013 when he met Kathie Lee Gifford at a restaurant event in Greenwich, Connecticut.

  • The Engagement (2020): In November 2020, Kathie Lee Gifford publicly announced her engagement to Joel Schiffman, celebrating their bond after seven years together.
  • Amicable Separation (2022): In early 2022, the couple announced that they had decided to call off their wedding engagement while remaining close friends. Both emphasized deep ongoing fondness, mutual appreciation, and respect for each other's individual life pathways.
