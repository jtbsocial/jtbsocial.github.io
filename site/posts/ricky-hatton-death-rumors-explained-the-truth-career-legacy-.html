Born in Stockport, Greater Manchester, Ricky Hatton became one of the most popular British boxers in modern history. Known for his aggressive, relentless body-punching style and unmatched charisma, Hatton commanded a fan base that regularly travelled by the thousands to Las Vegas.

The Breakthrough: Victory Over Kostya Tszyu (2005)

Hatton's defining moment inside the squared circle came in June 2005 at the Manchester Arena. Facing the formidable Kostya Tszyu—regarded as one of the pound-for-pound greatest light-welterweights of his era—Hatton produced a masterclass in pressure fighting. Tszyu retired on his stool before the 12th round, crowning Hatton as the world champion and instantly launching him into international superstardom.

The Las Vegas Conquests & Mega-Fights

Hatton captured the hearts of boxing fans globally, taking massive crowds of traveling supporters to Nevada for defining bouts against Floyd Mayweather Jr. in 2007 and Manny Pacquiao in 2009. While both fights ended in defeat, his willingness to step into the ring with the world's absolute best cemented his legacy as a warrior who never backed down from a challenge.

How to Spot Fake News and Death Hoaxes Online

With digital hoaxes on the rise, verifying news before sharing it online is essential. Use this framework when encountering sensational news about public figures:

  1. Check Reputable Outlets: Look for official statements from established organizations such as BBC, Sky Sports, or ESPN.
  2. Inspect the URL & Source: Ensure the website isn't spoofing a legitimate news outlet with subtle domain misspellings.
  3. Verify Social Profiles: Authentic updates usually come directly from verified personal accounts or official family representatives.
  4. Beware of Clickbait Headlines: Beware of dramatic headlines featuring vague language, urgency, or requests to click suspicious external links.
