Tactical Deep-Dive: Formations & Spatial Dynamics

Club Puebla: The Fluid 3-4-2-1 Asymmetric Build-up

In 2026, Puebla’s tactical framework centers on controlled chaos. Playing at Estadio Cuauhtémoc, La Franja utilizes a flexible 3-4-2-1 structure that transforms into an aggressive 3-1-5-1 during attacking phases.

  • Wing-Back Overloads: Puebla relies on rapid overlapping wing-backs to stretch Santos' compact central defensive blocks.
  • Half-Space Inverted Playmaking: The dual attacking midfielders operate almost exclusively in the half-spaces, pulling opponent central defenders out of position to open channels for late-arriving runners.
  • Pressing Triggers: Puebla triggers an all-out counter-press the second possession is lost in the middle third, aiming to punish Santos before their defensive structure sets.

Santos Laguna: The High-Octane 4-2-3-1 Vertical Attack

Santos Laguna remains steadfast in their commitment to lightning-fast vertical transitions. Los Guerreros build their game plan around explosive wing play and sudden central penetrations.

  • Direct Verticality: Upon winning possession, Santos averages just 3.8 passes before attempting a progressive pass into the final third.
  • Double-Pivot Shield: The two central defensive midfielders act as a defensive screen, utilizing real-time radio telemetry instructions from the bench to shift laterally and seal off passing lanes.
  • High Defensive Line: Santos pushes their back four high up the pitch to compress the playing area, forcing opponents into rushed long balls.
