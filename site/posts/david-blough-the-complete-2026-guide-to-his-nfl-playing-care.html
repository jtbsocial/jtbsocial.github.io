Born in Carrollton, Texas, David Blough demonstrated exceptional athletic ability and leadership qualities early on. Attending Creekview High School, Blough quickly emerged as a standout dual-threat quarterback in competitive Texas high school football. During his high school career, he amassed impressive statistics, displaying the poised pocket presence, quick decision-making, and pinpoint accuracy that would become signatures of his career.

By his senior year, Blough earned three-star recruit status and attracted nationwide attention from major collegiate programs. His commitment to Purdue University marked the beginning of a transformative era for both the player and the program.

At Purdue, David Blough experienced a collegiate career defined by perseverance, dramatic comebacks, and major offensive records. After redshirting his freshman year in 2014, Blough was thrust into starting action in 2015.

The Jeff Brohm Era and Offensive Explosion

The turning point of Blough's college career came with the arrival of head coach Jeff Brohm in 2017. Brohm’s aggressive, pro-style spread offense unlocked Blough’s potential as a field general. Despite enduring a severe ankle injury late in 2017, Blough returned in 2018 for a legendary senior campaign.

The Historic Upset over #2 Ohio State

On October 20, 2018, Blough delivered one of the most iconic individual performances in Purdue history. Facing the #2-ranked Ohio State Buckeyes, Blough threw for 378 yards and 3 touchdowns, leading the Boilermakers to a shocking 49-20 victory. The game put Blough's calm decision-making under pressure on national display and solidified his reputation as a big-game quarterback.

NFL Playing Career: The Gritty Professional Journey (2019–2023)

Despite his stellar college production, Blough went undrafted in the 2019 NFL Draft due to concerns over size and arm strength. However, his work ethic and quick mental processing earned him immediate opportunities in the pros.

1. Cleveland Browns & Detroit Lions (2019–2021)

Signed as an undrafted free agent by the Cleveland Browns in 2019, Blough was traded to the Detroit Lions prior to the regular season. When starters Matthew Stafford and Jeff Driskel suffered injuries, Blough was thrust into the spotlight on national television.

  • Thanksgiving Day Debut (2019): Starting against the Chicago Bears on Thanksgiving Day, Blough threw a 75-yard touchdown pass to Kenny Golladay on his first career completion. He finished with 280 passing yards and 2 touchdowns, earning widespread praise for his composure.
  • Backup Reliability: Over the next two seasons in Detroit, Blough earned deep respect in the quarterback room as a film-study wizard and trusted backup.

2. Minnesota Vikings & Arizona Cardinals (2022–2023)

Blough spent time on the Minnesota Vikings' practice squad before signing with the Arizona Cardinals late in the 2022 season. In Weeks 17 and 18 of 2022, Blough started two games for Arizona, completing over 65% of his passes and demonstrating a veteran command of complex playbooks.

Transition to Coaching: The Rapid Rise in Washington (2024–2026)

In early 2024, David Blough made a bold decision that shifted his football trajectory forever. Recognizing his elite analytical mind and knack for breaking down defensive coverages, the Washington Commanders hired Blough as their Assistant Quarterbacks Coach at just 28 years of age.

Mentoring Jayden Daniels

Working under head coach Dan Quinn and offensive coordinator Kliff Kingsbury, Blough was tasked with helping develop rookie quarterback Jayden Daniels, the No. 2 overall pick in the 2024 NFL Draft. Blough’s impact was immediate:

  • Tailored Drill Mechanics: Blough implemented specific footwork drills tailored to modern dual-threat quarterbacks.
  • Tablet & Film Mastery: Leveraging his recent playing experience, Blough translated complex NFL defensive schemes into easy-to-read pre-snap cues for Daniels.
  • Historic Rookie Season: Under the guidance of the staff, Jayden Daniels captured the 2024 NFL Offensive Rookie of the Year award, setting franchise records for completion percentage and total offensive output.
