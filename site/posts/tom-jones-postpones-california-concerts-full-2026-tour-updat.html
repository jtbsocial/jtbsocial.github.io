Music fans across the Golden State received unexpected news today as management for global icon Sir Tom Jones confirmed the postponement of his upcoming California performance dates scheduled for spring 2026. The 85-year-old vocal powerhouse, whose career spans over six decades, was slated to bring his highly acclaimed world tour to iconic venues across California.

While tour delays are never ideal, representatives confirmed that the decision was made out of an abundance of caution following advice from medical professionals. The postponement guarantees that Sir Tom returns to the stage at full strength, delivering the unmatched vocal range and raw energy fans have expected since his 1960s breakout.

Promoters have reassured fans that this is a temporary pause rather than a cancellation. Restructuring a stadium-level acoustic and visual tour across major West Coast venues takes complex coordination, but revised calendar slots have already been finalized.

The Logistics Behind Tour Rescheduling

Modern concert tours involving seasoned headliners require immense coordination. Shifting an entire West Coast leg involves several key factors:

  1. Acoustic and Production Rigs: Transporting state-of-the-art spatial audio dynamic speaker arrays and custom LED visual walls requires precise trucking and venue availability windows.
  2. Venue Availability: Legendary outdoor venues like the Santa Barbara Bowl and Los Angeles Greek Theatre maintain dense booking schedules. Aligning open dates across five major cities requires weeks of behind-the-scenes negotiations.
  3. Vocal Preservation: Delivering signature tracks such as "It's Not Unusual," "Delilah," and "Sex Bomb" requires peak physical condition. Taking adequate recovery time ensures Sir Tom's legendary baritone remains intact.

Point-of-Purchase Policies

  • Ticketmaster: Original buyers will receive an automated email detailing the 30-day refund window. Clicking the "Request Refund" link inside your account portal will send funds back to the primary payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • AXS: For venue bookings processed through AXS (such as select Santa Barbara or Saratoga dates), log into the AXS app to manage tickets directly. Refunds are available upon request during the standard 30-day grace period.
  • Third-Party Sellers (StubHub, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats): If you purchased through secondary marketplaces, your tickets remain valid for the new dates. If you cannot attend, you must list your tickets for resale on those platforms, as third-party vendors do not honor primary venue refund windows.
