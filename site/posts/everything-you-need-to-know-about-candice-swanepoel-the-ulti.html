From Flea Market to Paris Catwalks

Candice Swanepoel was born on October 20, 1988, in Mooi River, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa. Raised in a quiet rural setting, her life changed dramatically at age 15 when she was discovered by model scout Kevin Ellis at a flea market in Durban.

Within months, Swanepoel moved to Europe to pursue full-time modeling. Her rare combination of statuesque features, golden hair, and effortless athleticism immediately captivated casting directors across London, Milan, Paris, and New York.

Breaking into High Fashion

By the mid-2000s, Candice was walking for top-tier designer brands. Her early runway portfolio reads like a hall-of-fame directory of couture:

  • Givenchy (under Riccardo Tisci)
  • Versace (a favorite muse of Donatella Versace)
  • Chanel
  • Dolce & Gabbana
  • Oscar de la Renta
  • Fendi

Her ability to effortlessly switch between smoldering commercial appeal and strict high-fashion editorial aesthetics set her apart from her peers, earning her covers on international editions of Vogue, Harper's Bazaar, Elle, and GQ.

The Victoria's Secret Era: Becoming a Global Household Name

While her high-fashion editorial work gained critical acclaim, it was her partnership with Victoria's Secret that catapulted Candice Swanepoel into mainstream global pop culture.

Candice's signature runway walk—characterized by rhythm, confidence, and athletic elegance—became a defining element of the Victoria's Secret golden era. In 2013, wearing Mouawad's 10-million-dollar Royal Fantasy Bra featuring a 52-carat ruby centerpiece, her performance went down as one of the most memorable moments in fashion show history.

Tropic of C: Pioneering Sustainable Swimwear Entrepreneurship

Recognizing the need for a personal pivot that reflected her passion for nature and sustainability, Candice launched Tropic of C in 2018 as Founder and Creative Director.

The Eco-Conscious Brand Vision

Tropic of C is not merely a celebrity endorsement; it is a fully hands-on design and business endeavor. Inspired by nature, eco-conscious living, and body empowerment, the brand focuses on:

  1. ECONYL® Regenerated Nylon: Transforming ocean plastic waste, fishing nets, and industrial scraps into premium swimwear textiles.
  2. Low-Impact Manufacturing: Partnering with ethically certified artisan communities worldwide to reduce water waste and carbon footprint.
  3. Sculptural Minimalism: Timeless silhouettes engineered to fit diverse body types comfortably.

By 2026, Tropic of C has expanded beyond swimwear into bio-tech activewear, sustainable resort clothing, and luxury accessories, setting a benchmark for celebrity-founded sustainable fashion brands.

Fitness, Wellness, and Athletic Training Regimen

Candice Swanepoel's physique and glowing energy are famous worldwide. Unlike extreme quick-fix routines, Candice advocates for holistic, sustainable health routines tailored to strength and mental clarity.

Core Pillars of Candice's Fitness Routine

  • Pilates & Barre: Focuses on core stabilization, posture improvement, and long muscle toning.
  • Resistance & Weight Training: Incorporates ankle weights, resistance bands, and kettlebell squats to build glute strength and functional power.
  • Boxing: Used for high-intensity cardio, agility, stress relief, and shoulder definition.
  • Yoga & Breathwork: Daily practice for flexibility, mental grounding, and stress control during intense travel schedules.

Nutrition and Lifestyle Mindset

  • Whole Food Focus: Balanced diet rich in fresh organic vegetables, lean proteins, avocado, and nutrient-rich smoothies.
  • Hydration First: Coconut water and high-electrolyte hydration routines.
  • 80/20 Rule: Candice frequently emphasizes balance over strict restriction, enjoying pasta, bread, and dark chocolate in moderation.

Philanthropy, Motherhood, and Personal Life

Motherhood

Candice is a mother to two sons, Anacã and Ariel, whom she shares with model Hermann Nicoli. She frequently credits motherhood with giving her a deeper perspective on life, time management, and self-care.

Environmental & Social Advocacy

  • mothers2mothers Ambassador: Dedicated to preventing mother-to-child transmission of HIV in sub-Saharan Africa by training and employing women living with HIV.
  • Wildlife Conservation: Actively supports anti-poaching units and rhinoceros conservation charities across Southern Africa.
  • Eco-Fashion Advocacy: Speaks regularly at international fashion summits on circular economy textiles and supply chain accountability.
