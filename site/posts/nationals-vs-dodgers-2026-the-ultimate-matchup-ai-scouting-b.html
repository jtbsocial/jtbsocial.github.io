5. Strategic Blueprint: How the Nats Can Upset LA

For the Nationals to defeat the heavyweight Dodgers in a multi-game series, they must execute a tactical game plan grounded in four distinct parameters:

  1. Exploit the Running Game: The Dodgers' pitching staff features several high-effort deliveries with slow time-to-plate times (>1.35 seconds). Washington must leverage its elite sprint speeds to disrupt pitcher rhythm.
  2. Target Early Counts: LA’s bullpen utilizes put-away breaking pitches with two strikes. Early-count aggression against high-zone fastballs negates their pitch-design advantage.
  3. Shift Aggressively Against Pull-Heavy Sluggers: Utilizing micro-positioning field setups based on real-time batter tracking gives Washington the defensive edge against LA's left-handed power hitters.
  4. Maximize High-Leverage Relievers: Avoid traditional inning roles and deploy shutdown bullpen arms strictly based on match-up Leverage Index (LI) calculations.
